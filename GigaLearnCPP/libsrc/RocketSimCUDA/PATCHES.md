# Local patches to the vendored RocketSim-CUDA (against upstream 4ae73b3)
P0-1  CMakeLists.txt: removed the hard-coded Windows CUDA path and `lib/x64/cudart.lib`; `find_package(CUDAToolkit)` after `project()`, link `CUDA::cudart`.
P0-2  CMakeLists.txt: `POSITION_INDEPENDENT_CODE ON` on `rocketsim_cuda_core` (upstream's Python module failed to link on Linux without it).
P0-3  CMakeLists.txt: options RSC_BUILD_CPU_ORACLE / RSC_BUILD_TESTS / RSC_BUILD_BENCH / RSC_BUILD_PYTHON (python OFF); `cpu_readme_benchmark` and `unified_soccar_benchmark` not built (the latter includes MSVC <intrin.h>).
P0-4  CMakeLists.txt: ctest wiring with labels. `RSC_DifferentialIdleKnownFail` is WILL_FAIL on purpose: upstream's own fail-fast gate breaks on `idle` at tick 22 on sm_86 (angular velocity 1.36e-4 vs 1e-4). If it ever passes, the test fails and tells us the situation changed.
P0-5  benchmarks/mix_benchmark.cu (ours): one SimContext per game mode, all stepped each tick (the mixed-mode saturation measurements).
P0-6  SimContext::SetTeamLayout(team_mask) / GetTeamLayout(): team of car slot c is bit c of the mask (default 0xAA = upstream's c % 2). `init_single_car` and the three init/reset kernels take the mask; same-team cars get distinct kickoff spawn slots. Needed for 2v1 / 3v2 / 3v1 (upstream hard-wired alternation). Tests: tests/gpu/test_l6_team_layout.cu (4), test_l6_reset_isolation.cu (3).
P0-7  Per-car step event flags `step_bump / step_bumped / step_demo / step_demoed` (CarStateSoA, 4 uint8 pool slices), set in `resolve_car_pair_collision_device` for CROSS-TEAM contacts only (a demo also counts as a bump), cleared by SimContext::ClearStepEvents() once per agent step so they accumulate over tickSkip ticks. Mirrors RLGymCPP `_BumpCallback`. Tests: tests/gpu/test_l1_bump_demo_events.cu (4). Mutation check: 4/4 injected bugs caught (no team filter, demoed never set, mask ignored, clear skipped).
Note on the plan's "active-car mask": not needed. Each game mode gets its own SimContext with an exact car count, so there are no inactive slots.
P1-1  physics/ball_dynamics.cuh: cap the ball's angular speed at BALL_MAX_ANG_SPEED (6 rad/s) every tick, as RocketSim's Ball::_FinishPhysicsTick does. Upstream capped it only in the car-ball path; a rolling ball then spun up to its rolling speed and stopped losing speed to friction (996 vs 716 uu/s after 3 s at 1500). After: 1229.7 / 970.6 / 719.3 vs v2 1230.9 / 969.9 / 716.7. Test: tests/gpu/test_l1_ball_ground.cu.
P1-2  physics/contact_solver.cuh (`resolve_ball_arena_collision`): speculative contact. The ball now bounces on the tick it WOULD penetrate (as Bullet's contact threshold does), not one tick later; penetration push-out only for real penetration. Drop from z = 400: first impact tick 118 (v2 118, was 119), first peak 201.5 (v2 199.7, was 203.2). Known cost: ball_ceiling now 35.7 uu off at 2 s (was inside the 30 uu band) because the same early trigger applies to fast ceiling hits. Tests: same file; mutation check 2/2 caught (no cap, no speculation).
P1-3  physics/arena_sdf.cuh: THE GOAL MOUTH. Upstream's back wall (y = 5120) was solid in front of the net: the SDF's goal cavity only applied once the ball CENTER was past the goal line, so a ball flying or rolling at the net bounced off an invisible plane one radius short (about y = 5029) and open-play goals were impossible. Now, for |x| < 892.8 and z < 642.7 in front of the line, the nearest solid is the rim (posts and crossbar, zero thickness), at sqrt(dy^2 + e^2); the floor continues into the cavity. After: the ball crosses the goal line on the same tick as RocketSim v2 and v3 (62 / 62 / 106 for the three test shots) with the speed within 0.1%; the crossbar's farthest point matches (5898). Known gaps: posts are zero thickness (a post hit deflects differently, ball_goal_post fails its band) and the net's curved back wall is a flat box (irrelevant while episodes end at the goal line). Tests: tests/gpu/test_l1_goal_mouth.cu; mutation check (upstream SDF) caught.
P1-4  src/cuda/step_kernel.cu + physics/contact_solver.cuh: SIMULTANEOUS car-ball resolution. Upstream resolved the cars of an arena one after another, each against the ball the previous car had already moved, so in a 50/50 the LAST slot always won (symmetric 1v1 kickoff race: the ball ended 1400 uu toward blue's side on the GPU; RocketSim v2 and v3 leave it at ~(12,15)). Now every car resolves against the same pre-step ball (BallSnapshot) and the ball's position, velocity and spin deltas are summed once (BallDeltas), then clamped. Single-car hits are unchanged (Part A: carhit_*, bump_*, all pass as before; 34/43 vs v3, 36/43 vs v2 unchanged). After: symmetric diag and straight kickoff races both leave the ball centred (0,0) vs CPU (11,15), first hits on tick 256 / 315 vs CPU 257 / 316. Open: the sum can overshoot when a car squeezes the ball against a second car from the other side (not yet measured). Tests: tests/gpu/test_l1_contested_ball.cu; mutation check (sequential resolver) caught.
P1-5  Supersedes the contact part of P1-4. Summing the two cars' contact impulses (Jacobi) over-counted a ball squeezed between cars: in the first corrected 1v1 cell every kickoff stayed a standoff and kickoff episodes ran 25% longer than on the CPU. Now phase 0 detects every contact against the pre-step ball and sums only the RocketSim extra-hit impulses (additive in RocketSim too: `_velocityImpulseCache`) plus push-out, and phase 1 solves the contact (normal + friction) impulses with 8 Gauss-Seidel passes over the touching cars, alternating direction, on the evolving ball velocity (`solve_car_ball_contact_pass`), as Bullet's solver does. Result is slot-order independent and does not double-count. Checks: symmetric kickoff races leave the ball at (7,0)-(17,..) vs CPU (11,15); squeeze_equal ball y 0 vs CPU 0; an unequal-speed squeeze matches the CPU ball speed within 2% (1081 vs 1065 / 1056); Part A unchanged (34/43 vs v3, 36/43 vs v2); 13 of 13 GPU tests pass.
P1-6  Jump, double jump, dodge, flip, air control, turtle recovery and boost now follow RocketSim v3 (0.3.5), the CPU trainer's engine, not v2 (upstream's lineage). physics/car_dynamics.cuh, src/cuda/step_kernel.cu: tick-counted jump with no 0.62 first-ticks scale and v3's re-arm rules; jump/flip/air logic and the sticky force read LAST tick's wheel contact; quantized control inputs, UE3 sine-table dodge heading, pitch-cancel gate, dodge torque rounding; air control stays active during a flip; boosting forces full air throttle and air boost accel equals ground (config.h); the first boosting tick is free, a tick that ends the boost gives no thrust, 33.3/s; order jump -> turtle -> dodge -> air torque; flip damping runs first; wheel suspension velocities are refreshed after velocity impulses (suspension.cuh); speed limits apply at the START of a tick, so reported angular speed can exceed 5.5 (about 7.2 in a flip), as in v3; `flip_time` is now "time since the last flip began" and starts at 10 (types, sim_context resets, adapter mapping as in the v3 facade); `is_jumping` value 2 = hold broken. NOT ported: v3's per-tick velocity quantization. Upstream's tests/unit/test_challenger1_dodge_stress.cu uses the old air-control signature (not built by our umbrella).
P1-7  physics/contact_solver.cuh + step_kernel.cu: chassis-world contacts for a car with FEWER than 3 wheels down are solved Bullet-style (contacts found on the start-of-tick pose, speculative, 10 sequential-impulse passes with friction, no push-out, then positions integrate). On 3+ wheels the upstream solver is kept (`resolve_chassis_arena_collision_driving`): the new one made ramp driving worse. This gate is an engineering compromise, not something either CPU engine does.
P1-8  physics/contact_solver.cuh + step_kernel.cu: car-ball contact solved as Bullet does: one normal row and one friction row per car with ACCUMULATED impulses, friction direction fixed from the pre-solve slip, limit 2x the accumulated normal impulse, 10 iterations (all normal rows, then all friction rows), no speed clamp inside the solve. The earlier pass (P1-5) applied normal and friction together, never revised either, and stopped once the contact was separating, which deflected off-centre hits wrongly (aerial: car 52 uu and ball 54 uu from v3 after 1 s of flight; now 6 and 4). Tried a 1.6 uu contact threshold: mixed (better for a carried ball, worse for flips into the ball), not adopted.
P1-9  step_kernel.cu + contact_solver.cuh: car-ball contacts now follow Bullet's order: found on the START-of-tick pose, solved on this tick's velocities, and positions move with the solved velocities (the kernel has already integrated, so the contact's velocity change and push-out are carried into the positions afterwards; rotation is re-integrated from the start pose). The RocketSim extra hit impulse is computed from start-of-tick velocities and does not move positions in its own tick. First-touch ticks match v3 in 21 of 23 hit scenarios (before: mostly one tick early). The 1.9 UU Bullet contact threshold was tried again after this and not adopted (fewer matching ticks, worse 800 uu/s off-centre hits; better carried ball).
P1-10 step_kernel.cu: this tick's engine force, brake, steer angle and wheel friction are applied in this tick (v3); upstream followed v2 and applied them one tick later. Driving, steering and handbrake went from 5-15 uu off v3 at 1 s to 0.3 or less.
