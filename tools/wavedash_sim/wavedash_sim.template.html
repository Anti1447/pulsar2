How often does the grid hit the window?

For each tick skip: with the flip on the last boundary at or before first contact, how many of the grid's phases give a wavedash (this scenario). Chance of landing in a window w ticks wide with a random phase is w/skip.
Measured offline in the real engine (wd_phase.cpp; timing alignment only, nose-up fall with a flip available, boost on/off per decision, a perfect planner; an upper bound): no shaping → 25% at skip 8, 50% at skip 4 (= window 2 ticks / skip). With boost shaping over the last 24 ticks: 88% at skip 8 (94% with 48 ticks of lookahead) and 100% at skip 4 (81% with only 12 ticks). Boost moves the contact tick by about 10 ticks over 24 ticks of lookahead.

    What is and is not measured.
    Measured in RocketSim v3 (one geometry, front flip, 1000 uu/s): flip impulse 500 uu/s up to the 2300 cap, back flip −(533+0.35v); flip rotation 7.4 rad/s for 79 ticks; vertical damping ×0.65 per tick from tick 18 of the flip; pitch control 12.5 rad/s², max 5.6 rad/s, damping 2.8/s; flip availability (no timer without a jump, 1.25 s after one); press window 1–2 ticks before first wheel contact; nose-down never cancels. The toy does not reproduce the engine for flips pressed 3–8 ticks early (engine: airborne after 90 ticks; toy: nose/body strike), and body-strike speed loss is a toy friction model. The toy window is 0–1 ticks before the contact (engine 1–2).
