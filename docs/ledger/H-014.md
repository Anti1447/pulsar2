## Hypothesis (user-directed 2026-11-22)
The method (goal-only reward, external opponents, near-goal bootstrap) also works at a finer control rate, giving a bot with 4x the temporal resolution of the 15 Hz runs.
## Design
Fresh network, `--gco-fork-ts2`, folder `build/checkpoints_gco_fork_ts2`. tickSkip 2, actionDelay 1 (was 8 / 0 in the GCO runs). Horizons x4 so they are the same in seconds: gaeGamma 0.9969 -> 0.99922, goal critic 0.9994 -> 0.99985, gaeLambda 0.95 -> 0.9875 (lambda goes beyond the two gammas asked for; same compensation). Nexto + ChronosV2 served 0.30 (0.15 each) from step 0, Galactus and Dice held out. The bots are wrapped in `DecimatedOpponent` (one decision per 8 ticks, 7-tick latency) so they play as trained. Reset mix: fresh3's near-goal mix (NearGoal 0.30, far NearGoal 0.20, 0.10 each BallNearCar/AirDrill/AirPlay/Kickoff/Random) until the wall breaks; at 1B steps and every 250M after, `tools/gco_health/chain_ts2.sh` runs `ts2_read.py --check` and, when broken, writes `KICK_MIX` into the run folder and restarts on the kick mix (Kickoff 0.35, NearGoal 0.16 + far 0.08, BallNearCar 0.16, AirDrill 0.07, AirPlay 0.07, Random 0.11).
## Pre-registered wall rule (converted to per-second units)
Broken when, over the last 100M steps, 4 x the no-touch penalty per step > -0.0005. (Amended 2026-11-23: the original also required mean episode length > 40 s; with Nexto/Chronos served 30%, post-wall episodes average ~28 s (nexchron2), so that clause could never fire and was dropped. Episode length is printed for information.) Reference: fresh3 hit this by ~700M 15 Hz steps = 175M sim-equivalent steps at ts2... i.e. ~700M ts2 steps if the wall costs the same SIM TIME, but ~4x more if it costs the same number of decisions. 1B ts2 steps is only 1/4 of the game time of 1B at 15 Hz.
## Not comparable per row
Per-step panels (ball-touch ratio, goals/10k rows, penalty rate) read about 1/4 of their 15 Hz values at equal behaviour. `ts2_read.py` prints fresh3 at the same sim time.
## Caveats
Single seed. The packaged RLBot client assumes tickSkip 8 (src/RLBotMain.cpp) and needs updating before this bot can be playtested.
