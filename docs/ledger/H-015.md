## Hypothesis (user-directed 2026-11-23; revised from a 1.5x-width plan the same day, which was never run)
Added depth (with the residual blocks it needs) reaches the wall sooner and/or plays better at the same step count than the ts2 baseline. At 60 Hz credit travels over 4x as many steps, so depth is the more plausible lever than width.
## Design
`--gco-fork-ts2deep`, fresh, identical to H-014 (tickSkip 2 / delay 1, gammas x4, lambda 0.9875, Nexto+Chronos 0.30, near-goal mix, seed 123) except depth, widths unchanged: shared trunk 2 -> 4 layers, policy 4 -> 6, critic trunk 2 -> 4, critic and goal-critic heads 2 -> 4. Models.cpp turns each pair after the stem into a residual block ({W,W} gets none), so ts2's plain {W,W} stacks become stem + 1 block + plain (policy: stem + 2 blocks + plain): the lever is depth AND the residual connections it needs. Params: policy path 6.08M -> 10.85M, critic own 6.41M -> 6.57M (12.98M with its trunk), 30.4M in all (ts2: ~15.8M). Policy LR and miniBatchSize not retuned. Starts automatically when ts2 reaches 5B (chain_arch3.sh, second in the sequence), runs to 5B and stops. Reads every 250M against ts2 at the same step count (build/arch_health.log).
## Pre-registered read
Wall: the step at which 4 x no-touch penalty > -0.0005 (ts2_read.py --check) vs ts2; the seed spread of that step across fresh 15 Hz runs was 580-860M (about +-20%), so a shift below ~25% is unresolved. Also gauntlet mean win and first-touch at 1B, steps/s (cost), entropy and update magnitude (LR sanity). Single seed.
