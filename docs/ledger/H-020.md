Reward detectors that look at "the next step" (wavedash landing, flip-reset touch edge, air-touch cooldowns in steps) have NOT been re-audited for tickSkip 4; none was run through reward-scenario-test / reward-harness here.
- The value-side depth change and the tick change are made together (user's spec); there is no clean ablation of either.
