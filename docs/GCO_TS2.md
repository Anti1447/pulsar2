## Architecture comparison (H-014..H-017), current plan (2026-11-23)
Four arms, sequential, each to 5B steps: ts2 (control), ts2deep (all nets deeper), ts2critdeep (critic stack deeper only), ts2wide (all nets 1.5x wider); everything else identical, seed 123. Static kickoff-mix switch at 4B steps (marker `KICK_MIX`), no wall evaluation. Chain `tools/gco_health/chain_arch3.sh` (unit `gco-arch3`), logs `build/arch3_status.log` / `build/arch3_health.log`; final copies in `build/protected_ts2/<arm>_final_<ts>`; then the 1024-game gauntlet per arm and the cross-architecture round-robin (`tools/gco_health/arch_h2h.py`, `GGL_H2H_OPP="<dir>@<sw>x<sd>/<pw>x<pd>"`).
Revision 2026-11-23 (user-directed, supersedes the 2B / 1B-switch plan): every arm runs to 5B steps with the static kickoff-mix switch at 4B steps (1B on the denser mix), chain `tools/gco_health/chain_arch3.sh` (unit gco-arch3; logs `build/arch3_status.log`, `build/arch3_health.log`; gauntlet-1024 and the cross-arch matrix at the end). Why: at tickSkip 2 one step is 1/4 of a 15 Hz step, so the 1B switch was only ~250M fresh3-equivalent, well before the wall (fresh3 broke at 580-860M of ITS steps = ~2.3-3.4B ts2 steps); the first ts2 run (2B, switched at 1.007B) finished pre-wall with gauntlet 0 wins, and is quarantined at `build/checkpoints_gco_fork_ts2_KICKMIX2B_20261123` (final copy `build/protected_ts2/QUARANTINE_ts2_kickmix2B_final_2005284864`). ts2 was restarted from the pre-switch copy (step 1,001,843,712, near-goal mix, metrics and gauntlet log trimmed to that step). chain_arch.sh / chain_arch2.sh are superseded.
Caveats: single seed (a second seed is low priority, flagged for the user's review after the four runs); deeper arms add residual connections as well as depth; per-step panels are not comparable with 15 Hz runs; fresh3 reference is not like-for-like (externals served 30% in ts2).
